# Account icon sources

The marks identify the user's external accounts; they are not this site's logo.
SVG artwork is embedded locally in index.html so browsing the homepage does not
send requests to the brands or an external icon service.

- GitHub: official `GitHub_Invertocat_White.svg`, from
  https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip . Original path and white fill retained;
  white is an approved version for dark backgrounds.
  Guidelines: https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo . GitHub's trademark terms apply.
- Douyin account: the shared musical-note silhouette from
  https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/tiktok.svg .
  The musical-note path is rendered with a white face, cyan `#25F4EE` and
  red `#FE2C55` offset layers on the dark account button. The colored treatment
  is a local adaptation of the monochrome silhouette, not an official SVG asset.
  SVG source checked on 2026-10-06; this is an account link, not an endorsement.
- Bilibili: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/bilibili.svg .
  Brand color `#00A1D6`, source https://www.bilibili.com .

Bilibili color metadata was checked in
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/data/simple-icons.json
on 2026-10-05. Simple Icons is CC0; brand trademark terms still apply.
No filters, gradients or hover recoloring are applied to the marks. Custom account
labels still use a neutral text fallback rather than a mismatched platform logo.
